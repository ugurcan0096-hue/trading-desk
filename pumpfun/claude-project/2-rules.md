# My Trading Rules

Export this page to PDF and upload it to the TRADING DESK Project.

## Sizing
1. Max **2%** of account per trade.
2. Max **5** open positions.

## Entry: no entry if any of these fail
3. Top 10 holders (excluding bonding curve / LP) own **more than 25%**.
4. Dev holds **more than 5%**, or dev **sold in the first 10 minutes**.
5. More than **3 wallets bought in block 0** (bundle).
6. Token is **older than 6 hours** with no new ATH.
7. Dev or a top holder is on my **known ruggers / snipers** list.
8. Socials (X / TG / site) are **younger than the token** or missing.

## Exit
9. Take **50% off at 2x**.
10. Stop at **-35%**. No exceptions, no "let me give it a minute."

## Me
11. **No trades after 1 AM** my time.
12. After **2 losses in a row**: run the TILT prompt before the next trade.
13. Every trade goes in the journal **before** I close the terminal.
